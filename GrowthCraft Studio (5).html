How we work

From first call to first results

The same four steps for every project, so you always know what happens next.

  1. Understand

    We talk about your business, your customers and what a good month looks like.

  2. Plan

FAQ

Questions people ask before they start

What services do you offer?

Digital marketing: social media management and creatives, SEO, Google Business Profile management, Meta Ads and Google Ads. Website development: WordPress business websites, landing pages and e-commerce stores.

How do we get started?

Call or WhatsApp Manjunath and tell us about your business. We then send a written plan with the scope, price and timeline. Nothing starts until you agree to it.

Do I need a website before running ads?

Not always, but ads work better when people land on a clear page that makes it easy to call or enquire. If you do not have one, we can build a landing page for your campaign.

Can I update my WordPress website myself?

Yes. We build WordPress websites so that you can change text and images yourself, and we can show you how.

How will I know if the marketing is working?

We track the calls, messages and enquiries that come from your ads, Google listing and website, and share reports so you can see what is working.

Do you guarantee results?

No. Nobody can honestly guarantee rankings or sales. We set clear goals with you, track them, and tell you what is working and what we will change.
